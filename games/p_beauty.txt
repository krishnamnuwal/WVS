You are taking part in a decision task together with {n_others} other people. You will not know who they are and they will not know who you are.

Each of you chooses a number between 0 and 100 (0 and 100 are allowed). All the numbers are collected and their average is calculated. The winner is the person whose number is closest to two-thirds of that average. The winner receives a prize of {prize}. If several people are equally close, the prize is shared equally among them.

Everyone decides at the same time, without talking to each other. This is done only once.

Which number do you choose?
